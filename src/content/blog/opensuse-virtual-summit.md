openSUSE akan mengadakan **openSUSE Virtual Summit** pada 1 Mei. Semua waktu pada jadwal adalah Waktu UTC. Acara akan akan berlangsung di [opensuse.reqmagic.com](https://opensuse.reqmagic.com/). Anda dapat mendaftar di [events.opensuse.org](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSvirtsmt) pada 30 April.

Simak pembicara dari Indonesia, diantaranya:

1. [Anak Agung Bagus Arya Wiradarma](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSvirtsmt/program/proposals/3065) – *IT Risk Management Based on ISO 31000 and OWASP Framework using OSINT (Case Study: Election Commission of X City)*.
2. [Estu Fardani](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSvirtsmt/program/proposals/3077) – *Introduction Podman for Beginner*.
3. [Kukuh Syafaat](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSvirtsmt/program/proposals/3080) – *Translating Open Source Apps*.
