Gelaran openSUSE.Asia Summit 2018 akan dimulai beberapa hari lagi. 2018 ini merupakan tahun kelima setelah sebelumnya diselenggarakan di:

* Beijing, Cina, 2014  
  [https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia14](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia14)
* Taipei, Taiwan, 2015  
  [https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia15](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia15)
* Yogyakarta, Indonesia, 2016  
  [https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia16](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia16)
* Tokyo, Jepang, 2017  
  [https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia17](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia17)

Tahun ini, [openSUSE.Asia Summit 2018](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18) diselenggarakan bersamaan dengan [COSCUP](https://2018.coscup.org/en) dan [GNOME.Asia Summit](https://www.gnome.org/news/2018/04/gnome-asia-2018/) bertempat di [National Taiwan University of Science and Technology](http://www-e.ntust.edu.tw/) dengan lebih dari [20 trek](http://blog.coscup.org/2018/07/2018-full-programme.html). Sudah terbayang akan seberapa meriahnya acara ini? Konon tiket peserta sudah ludes terjual sebanyak 1400 tiket.

Tahun ini, openSUSE Indonesia berhasil mengirimkan 9 orang pembicara, yaitu:

1. Ahmad Romadhon Hidayatullah aka Rania Amina; "[Working and Contributing to Open Source Project via Graphic Design in openSUSE Tumbleweed](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2128)"
2. Tonny Adhi Sabastian; "[Single Sign On Services with Free/Open Source Software at Universitas Indonesia – Updating to CAS 5](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2092)"
3. Rahman Yusri Aftian; "[Nusantara Metode Input at openSUSE](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2170)"
4. Estu Fardani; "[How Jogja Become City of GNU/Linux User Friendly](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2095)"
5. Mohammad Rifki Affandi Z; "[Build your cloud file hosting using Nextcloud on openSUSE Leap 15.0](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2062)"
6. Didiet Agus Pambudiono; "[Deployment of multi node web server, database server and storage session server with Ansible](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2086)"
7. Mohammad Edwin Zakaria; "[Maintaining the Good Spirit – openSUSE Indonesia Community Experience](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2167)"
8. Kukuh Syafaat; "[openSUSE Leap & Flatpak](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2005)"
9. Yan Arief Purwanto; "[Having fun with KDE: create a Plasmoid](https://events.opensuse.org/conference/summitasia18/program/proposal/2152)"

Selain itu, 9 pembicara diatas, ada beberapa teman-teman dari Indonesia juga yang akan hadir seperti dari GNOME, [LIbreOffice-ID](https://libreoffice.id/) dan [KLAS](http://klas.or.id/).

Sampai jumpa di Taipei.
